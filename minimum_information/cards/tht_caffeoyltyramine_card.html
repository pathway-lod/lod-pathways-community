The gene and the reaction tiers rest on the same EC number, so they are not independent; the honest answer is the range 0.60 to 0.90. Narrowing it is the job of the calibration.

Read with care. One compound and one pathway, the development case, so the example illustrates the tiers and does not estimate how often they succeed. No MS/MS or standard; structures are hand-written. The fingerprint was compared knowing the answer, and the generic mass deltas were reinstated after the benchmark. Weights and cut-offs are drafts. Across the whole dataset, gene-side evidence alone did not prioritize orphan features better than chance.
