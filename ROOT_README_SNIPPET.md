### Evidence-aware falcarindiol pilot (proposed)

A companion [falcarindiol pilot](pilots/falcarindiol_evidence/README.md) demonstrates how published plant pathway knowledge can be curated as typed claims with biochemical and genetic evidence, and how separate transcriptomic module and temporal gene–metabolite findings can be linked to those claims without assigning unverified catalytic reactions. The pilot provides educational RDF/Turtle, SPARQL competency queries, SHACL shapes, a preliminary GPML diagram, synthetic statistical test cases, and provenance-aware tests. See [the concise curation guide](docs/evidence-aware-pathway-curation.md) and [candidate term requirements](specification/proposals/falcarindiol-omics-evidence.md). **Numerical multi-omics statistics are synthetic demonstration values, not measured results from the Jeon et al. study.**
