# Term placement in MIPRIMO

One page per term: where the term sits in the MIPRIMO ontology, as a diagram of the term and all its ancestors up to BFO. Arrows point from a term to its parent (*is a* for classes, *sub-property of* for relations). The column `placement` of [`ontology_terms.csv`](../ontology_terms.csv) links each row to its page.

All diagrams on one page, with search and a filter by source: <https://pathway-lod.github.io/lod-pathways-community/term_placement/>

Generated by `src/scripts/make_term_diagrams.py` in the MIPRIMO repository; do not edit by hand. Source: MIPRIMO commit 0f48473 (branch review-evidence-model).
Imported terms keep only their parents, not their other axioms. `MIPRIMO:` IDs use a placeholder namespace until the final one is chosen; they replace the `lodp:` placeholder terms.
